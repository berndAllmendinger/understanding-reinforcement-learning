# Data and content provenance

Prepared for Bernd Allmendinger's *Understanding Reinforcement Learning: Probability and Mathematics Explained Step by Step*, 1 November 2026.

## Original sources for this companion

- The author's approved manuscript v21 supplies the terminology, game conventions and teaching approach. The book itself is not bundled here.
- The Nim environment uses a single pile, legal removals 1–3, last removal loses, and the fixed take-two opponent by default. Random training starts cover 1–9.
- The Tic-Tac-Toe environment uses ordinary 3 × 3 rules, learner X first, and uniform legal O replies during training.
- All records are generated by these local environment functions. Training runs and hand-constructed episodes are explicitly distinguished. No customer simulation, observed industrial record or external game database is included.
- The approved cover is an unchanged copy of `Cover_Original_7x10_300dpi.png`, the white/petrol, broad-landscape front cover. Its 300 dpi export was interpolated from the approved smaller source; this repository does not claim newly captured image detail.
- Repository structure follows the author's [Quantitative Sports Betting companion](https://github.com/berndAllmendinger/quantitative-sports-betting). No sports datasets or sports model code were copied.

## Methodological references

- Sutton, R. S. and Barto, A. G. (2018). *Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction*, second edition. [Author's book page](http://incompleteideas.net/book/the-book-2nd.html). Monte Carlo, temporal-difference methods and policy gradients.
- Schulman, J., Wolski, F., Dhariwal, P., Radford, A. and Klimov, O. (2017). [Proximal Policy Optimization Algorithms](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06347). The clipped policy objective; the notebook uses an explicitly simplified tabular implementation with Monte Carlo advantages.
- [Google Colab FAQ](https://research.google.com/colaboratory/intl/en-GB/faq.html) and [official Colab URL redirect example](https://github.com/googlecolab/open_in_colab) informed the opening instructions.
- [GitHub file-upload documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/working-with-files/managing-files/adding-a-file-to-a-repository) informed the author upload guide.

Code and explanatory text were prepared with AI assistance for the author's companion project, executed locally, and subjected to the checks described in `REPRODUCIBILITY.md`. This is not an independent human peer review.

No external reference is copied as a chapter, figure or implementation. Cover and book rights remain separate from the MIT companion-code licence.
